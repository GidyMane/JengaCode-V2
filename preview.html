About JengaCode

A place where children build, not just watch

JengaCode runs practical digital skills programs for children and teens across Nairobi. We work with parents, schools, churches and community organisations to deliver hands-on learning in coding, robotics and digital literacy.

Turning tech curiosity into creative confidence

  • What we teach. Scratch, Python, robotics, artificial intelligence, web and game development, and the digital literacy that holds it all together.
  • How we teach. Small groups, one project at a time, with printed step-by-step workbooks the child keeps.
  • Why it matters. Coding teaches a child to break a big problem into small ones and keep going after something fails. That habit outlasts any single language.
  • Where we work. Holiday camps and weekend classes, plus in-school and after-school programs aligned with Kenya's Competency Based Curriculum.

Our programs

Seven pathways. One habit: build something real.

Children start where they are and move up as they are ready. Every pathway ends with a finished project your child can show you.

Scratch programming

Drag-and-drop blocks that behave like real code. Children build animated stories and their first playable game in the first few sessions.

Ages 8 to 11

Python

Their first typed language. Quizzes, calculators and small apps, written line by line so they can read their own code back to you.

Ages 11 to 17

Robotics

Build a robot that moves, senses and reacts. Motors, sensors and the code that drives them, tested against real challenges.

Ages 9 to 17

Artificial intelligence

How machines learn. Children train a simple image or sound model, then find out exactly where and why it gets things wrong.

Ages 12 to 17

Web development

HTML and CSS to a page that is genuinely theirs. Every child finishes with a live site they can open on your phone.

Ages 10 to 17

Game development

Characters, levels, scoring and sound. From a sketch on paper to a game their friends can actually play and lose at.

Ages 9 to 17

Digital literacy

Typing, files, research and staying safe online. The groundwork every other skill sits on, taught properly rather than assumed.

Ages 8 to 13

Why parents choose us

What you are actually paying for

Project based learning

No copying slides. Children learn by building something, breaking it, and fixing it.

Small class sizes

Groups stay small enough that an instructor reaches every child several times a session.

Experienced instructors

Trainers who work in tech and know how to explain it to an eleven year old.

Safe, supportive rooms

Supervised venues, clear drop-off and pick-up times, and a no-mockery rule when code fails.

Creativity and critical thinking

Children choose what to build. The thinking behind the choice matters as much as the code.

Future ready skills

Programming, robotics and AI literacy, aligned with the Competency Based Curriculum.

Certificate of participation

Every child who completes a camp or term receives a certificate and keeps their workbook.
